# Journey collage

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool on 2026-10-12. The final opaque paper illustration is used in the journey menu. Earlier transparent attempts were rejected because their edges contained a diffuse halo.

## Original prompt

Create one production illustration asset for ReMap, a premium iPad mind-mapping app. This is an isolated editorial paper collage for the small header of a study-journey menu, NOT a UI screenshot. Wide composition approximately 3:2 with generous 12% transparent margin. A folded cream paper map sits at a slight angle on a small irregular olive risograph patch. On the map a simple ink route connects five abstract spots, with a crisp small butter-yellow pennant at the end; in the foreground two layered cream index cards and one small monochrome halftone pencil cutout. Sophisticated Vox explainer visual language: tactile scanned paper fibers, restrained risograph grain, coarse monochrome halftone details, imperfect cut edges, confident minimal composition readable at 120px wide. Strict palette: cream #ECE8DF, off-white #F6F3EC, olive #A9A66B, dark olive #6F7040, butter #F3EDB0, ink #2B2E26. No readable text, letters, numbers, UI, buttons, labels or border. Flat lighting; no 3D rendering, gradients, glow, halo, vignette, blurred or soft shadow. Genuine transparent alpha background, no white rectangular sheet behind the composition and no checkerboard. Crisp isolated cutout.

## Final edit prompt

Make this a FLAT PRINTED EDITORIAL ILLUSTRATION on a completely solid uniform opaque warm cream #F6F3EC background. Preserve the arrangement of folded paper map, two cards, pencil and olive patch from the supplied reference. Remove all luminous halos, gradients, shadow, vignette, and shading outside the objects. Everywhere outside the crisp olive and paper objects must be the exact same flat solid #F6F3EC cream, including all four corners and all spaces surrounding every object. Preserve tactile paper fibers INSIDE the objects only. No transparency. No dark edges. No white glow. No text. Looks like ink and flat paper pasted onto one uniform cream sheet, scanned without any shadows. Wide 3:2 composition.
