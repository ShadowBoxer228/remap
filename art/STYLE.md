# ReMap illustration style — "Vox explainer collage"

Look: editorial collage like Vox explainer animations. Black/olive-gray HALFTONE-DOT photo cutouts
(coarse visible dot screen), grainy risograph/stipple texture on flat color shapes, cream paper,
hand-drawn thin ink doodles (circles connected by lines, squiggles, arrows), slightly imperfect
cut-paper edges. Flat lighting, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no photorealistic color.

Palette (strict): cream paper #ECE8DF, off-white sheet #F6F3EC, olive/khaki grain #A9A66B,
dark olive #6F7040, butter yellow #F3EDB0, ink #2B2E26. Halftone photos are monochrome
ink-on-cream (no other colors).

Motifs: mind maps as circles linked by lines; grainy olive "cloud" blobs; paper sheets;
hands; pencils; students; books. Calm, smart, a little playful.

Rules: no readable text unless specified; isolated subject; generous empty margin around the
subject; transparent background when requested (true alpha, no checkerboard, no paper behind).

HARD RULE: no glow, no halo, no soft outer shadow, no vignette around cutouts. Edges must be crisp,
slightly irregular cut-paper edges directly against the transparent background.

Place: the team is in Istanbul — architecture motifs should be Istanbul landmarks (Galata Tower,
Beyoğlu row houses, mosque domes and minarets, Bosphorus ferries, seagulls), not generic European campuses.
